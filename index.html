How these numbers are counted

Counts are unique CSPs from the 169 in the campaign list, read live from the in-app's tracking sheet each time this page loads (and every minute while it stays open). Test opens and opens with no CSP id are left out. Resumed or declined is the CSP's latest tap on the first screen. "Ticked, not sent" counts reasons ticked by CSPs who skipped or left the reason form. Phone numbers inside typed reasons are masked. CleverTap figures are entered by hand from the campaign pages.
